Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ScientiaForge and the AI Interview Simulator.

How does the AI Interview Simulator work?

The simulator recreates real-world interview conditions. It poses industry-specific or behavioral questions, evaluates your answers in real time, and breaks down your performance based on structure, key terminology, and confidence factors.

Is ScientiaForge designed for students and entry-level professionals?

Yes! ScientiaForge is built specifically to bridge the gap between education and employment, addressing the exact realities and time constraints faced by modern learners and young professionals.

How do I add my team photos to the "Meet the Team" page?

In the `about.html` file, find the block marked `class="team-photo-container"`, ensure your image files are in the same folder, and verify the filename is mapped correctly.

Is my practice data kept private?

Your practice sessions and performance metrics are secure and designed to help you benchmark your personal progress over time without unwanted third-party data tracking.
